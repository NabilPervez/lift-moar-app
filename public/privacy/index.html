Lift More Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

The short version: Lift More has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking. Your workouts, templates, schedule and bodyweight are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

This policy covers the Lift More: Track & Visualize app, including the Android app on Google Play and the web app at lift-more.netlify.app (together, “the app”). The app is made by Nabil Pervez.

Information stored on your device

Everything you enter stays in the app’s local storage on your phone or browser. That includes:

  • workout templates, your weekly schedule and custom exercises
  • logged workouts: exercises, sets, weights, reps, RPE and notes
  • bodyweight entries
  • app settings such as your theme

We cannot see, access or recover this information. It is not uploaded to any server we run. There is no Lift More server that stores user data.

Information we do not collect

The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or health-platform data. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not sell or share personal information.

Services the app relies on

  • Hosting (Netlify). The app’s files are served by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify receives standard request information such as your IP address and browser type when the app is loaded. See Netlify’s privacy policy.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts). The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts are downloaded. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Android app. The Android app opens the web app inside your device’s browser engine (usually Chrome), so that browser’s own privacy settings also apply.

Sharing a workout

When you choose to share a finished workout, by copying it, sending a text, sending it through WhatsApp or saving it to a file, the app prepares the text and hands it to the app you pick. Nothing is sent unless you complete that step yourself, and the app you share with handles the message under its own privacy policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses them only to tell you when a rest timer ends. They are created on your device and are not sent through any server.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual workouts, clear all workout history from Settings, or remove everything by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it. Because we never hold a copy, clearing storage or uninstalling deletes the data permanently.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
